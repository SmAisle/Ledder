How to Play Ledder

GoalOne board a day. Score points. Earn 3 stars.
StartYour first word starts in the top row.
SpellMove one row at a time, to a letter straight ahead or one tile over.
Next wordStarts on your last letter. The rest of that row locks.
Rows 3 to 5: go up or down.
Rows 2 and 6: turn back.
UnlockReach the top or bottom row to unlock the board. Your next word can start on any letter.
